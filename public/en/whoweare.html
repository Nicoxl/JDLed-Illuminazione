Who We Are

Our story is rooted in the experience and professionalism that have always set this lighting shop apart. Under new management, we aim to continue upholding the excellence and passion for lighting that, for years, have made Benetti Illuminazione a leading name in the city and the Agno Valley region.

Today, with a refreshed perspective on the latest design and technology trends, we maintain the values of competence, seriousness, and customer attention that have defined us. We offer a carefully selected range of chandeliers, wall sconces, floor lamps, and lighting solutions for every style and need, along with consultation services to help you choose the perfect lighting for your home or business.

We are here to welcome you, advise you, and illuminate every one of your projects with quality and professionalism. We look forward to seeing you!
